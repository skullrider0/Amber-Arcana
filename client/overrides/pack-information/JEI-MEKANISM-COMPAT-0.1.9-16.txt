Amber & Arcana 0.1.9-16 — Mekanism / JEI compatibility hotfix

Client recipe viewer: JEI 15.2.0.27 (CurseForge project 238222, file 4712868)
Dedicated server: JEI intentionally omitted.

Why: Mekanism 10.4.16's Minecraft 1.20.x build uses JEI 15.2.0.27 as its dependency baseline. Newer JEI builds can leave Mekanism items visible while its machine recipe categories do not register.

Acceptance check after a FRESH CurseForge import:
1. Launch and join the matching server.
2. Search JEI for Atomic Alloy.
3. Press R / view recipe.
4. A Mekanism Metallurgic Infuser recipe category should appear.
5. Check at least one Crusher, Enrichment Chamber, and Energized Smelter recipe too.

Atomic Alloy is a Mekanism machine recipe made by infusing Reinforced Alloy with the Refined Obsidian infusion type in a Metallurgic Infuser.
